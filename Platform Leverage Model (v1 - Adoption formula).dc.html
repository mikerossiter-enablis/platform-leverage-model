Where DORA starts to diverge
2 of 5
DORA metrics reward activity.
Deployment frequency · lead time

We ship constantly too — but those two describe your customers' success, not yours.

Deployment frequency and lead time were built to score product delivery. DORA's own research shows platform work tends to pull against those throughput numbers — the wrong ruler for the layer underneath.

Act III · The model
12
Keep them in mind — don't chase them

A lens, not a target.

When a measure becomes a target, it stops being a good measure.

Goodhart's Law

Game fan-out or chase an adoption percentage and you've missed the point. A lens to guide judgement, not replace it — not a scoreboard, and never a stick to beat anyone with.
