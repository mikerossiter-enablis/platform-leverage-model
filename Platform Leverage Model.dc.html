Part three · The model
15

The five things.

Team level. Whatever the team runs.
01 Reach Grow ↑ How widely it’s actually used — only counts if they could say no.
02 Depth Grow ↑ How much more a team can do because you exist.
03 Cognitive load Shrink ↓ The thinking you force on the people you serve.
04 Toil Shrink ↓ The manual grind on your own side.
05 Reliability The multiplier Whether people can trust it. Nought to one.
It can go negative

Load + toil outweigh reach × depth: a net drain. Never become that.

Part five · The takeaway
18

When anyone asks you for something.

The request is reactive. The answer needn’t be.
Reach Who else is going to need this?
Depth What will it let them do that they couldn’t before?
Load How much will we have to hold in our heads?
Toil What’s left for us to do by hand next time?
Reliable Does it stay trustworthy?
