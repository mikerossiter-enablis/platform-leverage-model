Leverage is the output — not an input
Leverage = Reliability ×
[ Reach × Depth − ( Cognitive Load + Toil ) ]
GROW ↑ SHRINK ↓ The floor · Reliability 0→1
Grow these. Shrink these. Never breach the floor.
enablis
Altitude 2 · Model
16

The five things.

Team-level — no job titles required. One small team, Windows & network through to Terraform & AWS.
01
Reach
Grow ↑

How widely it's genuinely used.

e.g. one backbone everyone builds on.

02
Depth
the real prize
Grow ↑

How much more a team can do because you exist.

e.g. 6 weeks + 3 teams → an afternoon.

03
Cognitive load
Shrink ↓

The weight you put on those you serve.

measure: time-to-provision · steps · self-service.

source: Team Topologies — Skelton & Pais.

04
Toil
Shrink ↓

The manual grind on your own team.

SRE: keep it under half your time.

05
Reliability
The multiplier

SLOs and error budgets — 0 to 1.

None of it counts if you're not trusted.

It can go negative

Load + toil outweigh reach × depth → negative. Never become that.

The team / platform, the estate (cloud + on-prem / legacy), and what was being measured before.

Which of the five — self-service to cut cognitive load, automation to cut toil, an SLO to set the floor, a paved path to add reach & depth.

03 · What improved

Before / after — toil down, time-to-provision down, fewer incidents, teams doing things they couldn't before. Honest and specific.
