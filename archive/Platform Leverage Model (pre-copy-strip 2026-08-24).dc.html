Where DORA starts to diverge

One metric is the real misfit — it rewards activity.
DEPLOYMENT FREQUENCY

We ship constantly too — but deployment frequency describes your customers' success, not yours.

Deployment frequency was built to score product delivery. You can run a rock-solid platform and deploy to it rarely — more isn't better when you're the foundation. The wrong ruler for the layer underneath.

Altitude 2 · Model — the reveal
15
Leverage is the output — not an input
Leverage = Reliability ×
[ Reach × Depth − ( Cognitive Load + Toil ) ]
GROW ↑ SHRINK ↓ The floor · Reliability 0→1
Grow these. Shrink these. Never breach the floor.

A mental model, not a sum. Don't plug numbers in — it's there to shape judgement.

enablis
Altitude 2 · Model
16

The five things.

What each one means — with an example.
01
Reach
Grow ↑

How widely it's genuinely used.

e.g. one backbone everyone builds on.

02
Depth
the real prize
Grow ↑

How much more a team can do because you exist.

e.g. 6 weeks + 3 teams → an afternoon.

03
Cognitive load
Shrink ↓

The weight you put on those you serve.

measure: time-to-provision · steps · self-service.

source: Team Topologies — Skelton & Pais.

04
Toil
Shrink ↓

The manual grind on your own team.

SRE: keep it under half your time.

05
Reliability
The multiplier

SLOs and error budgets — 0 to 1.

None of it counts if you're not trusted.

It can go negative

If load + toil outweigh reach × depth, leverage turns negative — a platform so painful and so heavy the org would be better off without it. Never become that.

enablis
Altitude 2 · Model
17
Keep them in mind — don't chase them

A lens, not a target.

When a measure becomes a target, it stops being a good measure.

Goodhart's Law

Game your reach number or chase a toil percentage and you've missed the point. A lens to guide judgement, not replace it — not a scoreboard, and never a stick to beat anyone with.

Altitude 3 · Triage — what you do Monday
18

Pick the worst pain. Take one step.

A compass, not a finish line — and no cloud, or full platform, required to start.

Find yourself here on Monday morning → do this one thing
Drowning in manual work Automate one repetitive job. Toil ↓
Teams waiting days on you Make one thing self-service. Cognitive load ↓
No idea if you're reliable Put one SLO on one service. Reliability
Can't even see the work Start a ticket queue — just begin to see it. Visibility
The cloud is a tool

— not the goal. Good method on the kit you've got beats bad method on shiny kit. Start where you are.
